# Symmetrical Super Learning

Summary:
The manuscript includes three experiments; two conducted by Georgina Georgina M. Aisbit, and the last one by Gabriella FitzGerald and Santiago Castiello. Simulations of the experiments were conducted with Rescorla-Wagner model by Santiago Castiello.

Reference:
Castiello, S., FitzGerald, G., Aisbitt, G. M., Baker, A. G., & Murphy, R. (2024). Symmetrical ‘Super Learning’: Enhancing learning using a bidirectional probabilistic outcome. Journal of Experimental Psychology: Animal Learning and Cognition.

List of content files:
-data (folder): processed data is here, ready for analysis and plots 
-figures (folder): figures are here and will be saved here
-simulations (folder): simulations are here
-functions.R (R functions are here)
-superSymmetryStats_v3.R (all stats are here)
-superSymmetryVisualization_v2.R (all visualizations are here)
